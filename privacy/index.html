Privacy Policy

Cat Number Match (Android, pro.vlasiichuk.cat_number_match) · Last updated: 13 October 2026

1. Who is responsible

Julian Vlasiichuk (vlasiichuk.pro)
Email: catnumbermatch@gmail.com

"We" means the developer named above. "The game" means the Android app Cat Number Match.

2. Short version

  • The game is for people aged 13 and over.
  • The game has no account and no login, and we run no server.
  • Your progress, coins, your cat and your settings are saved only on your phone.
  • The current version does not use the internet at all: it sends nothing to us or to anyone else.
  • We do not know your name and do not ask for it. The cat's name stays on your phone.

3. What stays on your phone

Saved in Android's storage for the game: the levels you cleared and their stars, coins, items, your cat (name, fur and eye colour), the cat's needs, your streak and daily calendar, your settings, and the date and time marks the game needs to count days.

This data is not sent to us. Uninstalling the game, or "Clear data" in Android's settings for the game, deletes it.

Android backup. If you have Android's backup turned on, Android may copy the game's save to your Google account and restore it on a new phone. This is done by Android and Google under your Google account settings, not by us. You can turn it off in your phone's settings. Google's privacy policy applies: policies.google.com/privacy.

4. Reminders (notifications)

If you say yes, the game books reminders (at most two a day, at about 6 pm and 9 pm) on your phone. They do not come from a server. You can switch each kind off in the game's settings or in Android's notification settings. Android 13 and newer ask you before the first one.

5. Ads, analytics and purchases

The current version has no ads, no analytics and no purchases. The "Test video" page in the game is a placeholder that shows nothing from outside.

A later version is planned to add ads (Google AdMob), anonymous usage numbers (Google Firebase Analytics, only after you agree) and purchases through Google Play. Before such a version is published, this page will be updated to explain what is collected, why, and how you can say no or change your choice in the game's settings.

6. Who gets data

Nobody: the current version does not send data anywhere. (Android's own backup, see section 3, is run by Google under your Google account.)

7. How long data is kept

On your phone, until you uninstall the game or clear its data.

8. Your rights

Under the GDPR you may ask for access, correction, deletion, restriction and portability of your data, object to its use, and withdraw consent at any time. Because the game keeps everything on your phone and has no account, we hold no data about you; you can delete it yourself as described in section 3. For questions, write to us at the email above. You may also complain to a data protection authority.

9. Children

The game is not meant for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you think a child under 13 uses the game, please write to us.

10. Changes

We update this page whenever the game changes what it does with data. The date at the top shows the latest change.
