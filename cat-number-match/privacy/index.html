2. Short version

  • The game is for people aged 13 and over.
  • The game has no account of its own, and we run no server. Your progress, coins, your cat and your settings are saved on your phone.
  • The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. In the EU, the UK and Switzerland Google asks for your choice first; you can change it any time in the game's Settings ("Ad choices").
  • Anonymous usage numbers (Google Firebase Analytics) are sent only if you agree. The game asks once; "Don't agree" is just as easy, and the game works the same either way. Change it any time in Settings ("Privacy choices").
  • Purchases (Remove Ads, coin packs) go through Google Play. We never see your payment details.
  • Optional: Google Play Games for achievements.

3. What stays on your phone

Saved in Android's storage for the game: the levels you cleared and their stars, coins, items, your cat (name, fur and eye colour), the cat's needs, your streak and daily calendar, your settings, your answers to the privacy questions, the codes Google Play gives for your purchases (so a purchase is never given twice), and the date and time marks the game needs to count days.

This data is not sent to us. Uninstalling the game, or "Clear data" in Android's settings for the game, deletes it.

Android backup. If you have Android's backup turned on, Android may copy the game's save to your Google account and restore it on a new phone. This is done by Android and Google under your Google account settings, not by us. You can turn it off in your phone's settings.

4. Reminders (notifications)

If you say yes, the game books reminders (at most two a day, at about 6 pm and 9 pm) on your phone. They do not come from a server. You can switch each kind off in the game's settings or in Android's notification settings.

5. Ads (Google AdMob)

The free version shows ads: videos you choose to watch for a reward (always optional), a full-screen ad after every third completed level (never at the start, never during play), and a small banner on the road map and in the cat room. Buying "Remove Ads" removes the ads that show by themselves; reward videos stay your choice.

Google AdMob, run by Google Ireland Limited, may use your device's advertising ID, your IP address, general device information and how you interact with ads, to show ads, measure them, prevent fraud and, if you agree, personalise them. In the EU, the UK and Switzerland, Google's consent form asks you before any ad is loaded; in some US states you can say "Don't sell or share my data". You can change your choice at any time in the game's Settings ("Ad choices"), and reset or delete your advertising ID in Android's settings.

Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR) for personalised ads and storing information on your device; our legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) in funding a free game for non-personalised ads where the law allows it. Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy; how Google uses data from apps: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

6. Anonymous usage numbers (Google Firebase Analytics)

Only if you tap "Agree". Before you answer, after "Don't agree" and after you switch it off, nothing is sent (Firebase itself also stays switched off).

What is counted: which levels are started and finished, how many stars, hints, undos and "Add numbers" were used, coins earned and spent and on what, which screens and features are used (shop, cat room, daily puzzle, settings switches), which videos were watched, and general device and app information (device model, Android version, language, country, app version) with a random app-instance ID. Not: your name, your cat's name, free text, your contacts or your location beyond the country.

Why: to see which levels are too hard or too easy, whether the coins are fair, and which features players use, so we can improve the game.

Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), which you can withdraw at any time in Settings ("Privacy choices"); withdrawing also deletes the usage data waiting on your phone. Firebase is run by Google Ireland Limited; data may be processed in the USA under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. Data is kept by Google for 14 months, then deleted.

7. Purchases (Google Play)

Purchases are handled entirely by Google Play. We receive only what Google Play tells the game: which product was bought, Google's purchase code and order number, and whether the payment is complete. We never see your name, card or payment details. Google's terms and privacy policy apply to the payment.

8. Google Play Games (achievements)

If you use Google Play Games, Google signs you in to its service and keeps your achievements in your Google account. This is Google's service under your Google account settings; we receive no personal data from it.

9. Who gets data

Google (AdMob, Firebase Analytics if you agreed, Google Play, Google Play Games), as described above. We do not sell your data and do not share it with anyone else.

10. How long data is kept

On your phone: until you uninstall the game or clear its data. At Google: see sections 5 to 8 and Google's policies.

11. Your rights

Under the GDPR you may ask for access, correction, deletion, restriction and portability of your data, object to its use, and withdraw consent at any time (in the game: Settings, "Ad choices" and "Privacy choices"). Because the game has no account and we run no server, we hold no data that names you; the data Google processes is linked to a random ID, not to you. For questions, write to us at the email above. You may also complain to a data protection authority.

12. Children

The game is not meant for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children. Ads in the game are limited to content suitable for teenagers. If you think a child under 13 uses the game, please write to us.

13. Changes

We update this page whenever the game changes what it does with data. The date at the top shows the latest change.
