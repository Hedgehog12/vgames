A short break should feel like one.

  • A break with zero pressure

    No timer, no lives, no getting stuck. Take all the time you want: every board is proven clearable, and "Add numbers" gives you more pairs when you run out.

  • A workout for your focus

    Spotting pairs, looking ahead, staying with one thing: a gentle exercise for your attention. Three minutes is enough, and the levels grow from easy to a real challenge.

  • No ad in the middle of a level

    Never. The first area has no ads at all. Later they come only between levels, and one purchase removes them for good.

Kind, not punishing

Wrong pair? Your cat tells you why.

Tap two numbers that don't fit and your cat explains in plain words. No lives, no timer, no penalty. Undo takes back a mis-tap, and hints come back as you play.

A reason to smile

A cat that is happy to see you.

Name it, pick its fur and eyes, and it cheers every match from above the board. Feed it, pet it, play with it. It never gets sick and it never runs away, and a happy cat earns you 20% more coins.

Something to come back to

A new puzzle every day, and a world to travel.

A daily puzzle, the same for everyone. A streak that forgives a missed day. And 50 cities on the road map, from Paris to Kyoto, each with its landmark and its own outfits for your cat.

Good to know

Is it really free? And the ads?

Yes. Every level, the daily puzzle and the cat are free. The first area has no ads at all. After you have cleared level 10, an ad shows between levels (after every third win) and a small banner on the map and in the cat room, never in the middle of a puzzle. Remove Ads is an optional one-time purchase, no subscription.

Do I need the internet?

No. The game works fully offline, needs no account, and your progress is saved on your phone.

Is it hard?

It starts gently and gets harder level by level, up to a real challenge. Every board can be cleared, and hints, undo and "Add numbers" are there when you need them.

What if I don't play for a few days?

Nothing bad happens. The game waits for you. Your cat may get a little hungry, and a free treat always works. A streak freeze can save your streak for a missed day.

Is it easy on the eyes and the battery?

There is a dark mode for the evening, and while you think, nothing on screen moves, so it is light on your battery.

Is there an iPhone version? Is it for children?

Not yet, Cat Number Match is for Android first. It is made for ages 13 and up.

Who makes it?

Julian Vlasiichuk, a one-person studio at vlasiichuk.pro. Questions and ideas are welcome at catnumbermatch@gmail.com.
