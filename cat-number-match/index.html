Good to know

Is it really free? And the ads?

Yes. Every level, the daily puzzle and the cat are free. There are ads, but they are set up so they don't get in the way of the game. If you prefer none, Remove Ads is an optional one-time purchase, no subscription, and the paid version has no ads.

Do I need the internet?

No. The game works fully offline, needs no account, and your progress is saved on your phone.

Is it hard?

It starts gently and gets harder level by level, up to a real challenge. Every board can be cleared, and hints, undo and "Add numbers" are there when you need them.

What if I don't play for a few days?

Nothing bad happens. The game waits for you. Your cat may get a little hungry, and a free treat always works. A streak freeze can save your streak for a missed day.

Is it easy on the eyes and the battery?

There is a dark mode for the evening, and while you think, nothing on screen moves, so it is light on your battery.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not yet. Cat Number Match is for Android first.

Is it for children?

The game is set up for players aged 13 and up.
